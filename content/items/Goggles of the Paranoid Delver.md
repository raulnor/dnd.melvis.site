---
title: Goggles of the Paranoid Delver
---
_Wondrous Item, Uncommon (Requires Attunement)_

While wearing these dark lenses, you gain the following benefits:

- You have [Darkvision](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Darkvision) out to 60 feet. If you already have Darkvision, wearing the goggles increases its range by 60 feet.
- You can cast [Detect Magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spell-descriptions#DetectMagic) at will (the visual overlay appears in your field of vision).
- You have advantage on [Investigation](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/playing-the-game#Skills) checks to find hidden doors, traps, or concealed objects

*NOTE: These are an upgraded version of [Goggles of the Night](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/magic-item:goggles-of-night) for my campaign where I require more magic items to have an attunement slot.*
