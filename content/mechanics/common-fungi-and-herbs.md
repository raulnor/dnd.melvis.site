title: "Common Fungi and Herbs"
---

*Those widely known for their practical or magical properties.*

<div class="two-column"><div class="column">

***Duration.*** All persistent effects last 1 hour (six turns) unless otherwise noted.

***Foraging.*** Once per day while traveling. Check may be rolled at advantage if party foregoes movement. DC 10 Survival for random herb (d100). DC 15/20/25 for specific herb (see table). 1d6 rations or 1d4 plants are foraged on success. Amount is +2 in fall, -2 in winter (minimum of 1).

</div><div class="column">

***Psychedelics.*** The potency of psychedelic fungi decreases with overuse. If used multiple times, a DC 5 Luck roll is required or the item has no effect. This number increases by 5 with each use and decreases by 5 each long rest.

***Value.*** All herbs have a value in gold pieces. Merchants will normally sell at this price and buy at half.

<div class="two-column"><div class="column">

1. **Arrowhame:** Tiny, dried leaves of a climbing plant. Grants [CON Save](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#SavingThrow) proficiency when rehydrated and consumed for 1 hour.

2. **Blood Canker:** Rust-red dried residue of a slime-mould prevalent in Mulchgrove. Heals 2d4 hp when mixed in liquid and drunk; DC 12 CON save or [Poisoned](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#PoisonedCondition) for 1 hour. When mixed with water, drink keeps for long periods. Shake before drinking.

3. **Bosun's Balm:** Roots dredged up from a rare river weed. Grants +5 to Stealth checks when eaten for 1 hour.

4. **Fenob:** The root-bulbs of a forest flower. Grants [Aid](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:aid) for 24 hours when placed under the tongue before bed.

5. **Gillywort:** A finely ground, white powder derived from the leaves of a creeper that favours dank cave-mouths. Casts [Detect Poison and Disease](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:detect-poison-and-disease) on up to 1 gallon of liquid when added. Liquid turns a shocking purple if test is positive.

6. **Grue's Ear:** Rubbery, pink ear fungus. Grants Advantage on Initiative rolls when consumed for 1 hour. *Psychedelic.*

7. **Hogscap:** Mottled, fist-sized puffballs, harvested in their plump, immature stage. Grants [Detect Magic](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:detect-magic) when consumed for 1 hour. *Psychedelic.*

8. **Lankswith:** Powdered root of a floating pond weed. Removes the [Poisoned](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#PoisonedCondition) condition when taken with a heated fluid.

9. **Lilywhite:** Carefully dried and folded petals of a marsh lily. Casts [Sleep](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:sleep) (DC 13) when smoked or ingested.

10. **Marshwick:** The seeds of a scarce and unremarkable marsh flower, ground into a blue powder. Grants [DEX Save](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#SavingThrow) proficiency when mixed in liquid and drunk for 1 hour.

</div><div class="column">

11. **Moonhaw:** Pale white berries of a thorny bush, only harvested at night under the light of the moon. Grants or increases [Darkvision](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#Darkvision) by 30 ft when chewed for 1 hour.

12. **Ofteritch:** A black liquid fermented from the young roots of the lantern elm. Casts [Purify Food and Drink](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:purify-food-and-drink) when added to one ration or meal.

13. **Sallow Parsley:** Silvery-leafed young shoots of a rare fern, which look uncannily like the culinary herb parsley. Grants 2 additional hit dice during a short rest when drunk as infusion. *Psychedelic.*

14. **Smottlebread:** Spongy, green, bread-like hunks of giant mushroom flesh. Grants [WIS Save](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#SavingThrow) proficiency when consumed for 1 hour. *Psychedelic.*

15. **Spirithame:** Delicate, curled leaves of a rare moss. Heals 2d8 hp when crushed and applied to wounds. Can only be used once per day.

16. **Tom-a-Merry:** Tiny, cute mushrooms as tall as a baby's finger, with pointy blue caps. Casts [See Invisibility](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:see-invisibility) when consumed for 1 hour. *Psychedelic.*

17. **Wallowmost:** Fluffy yellow buds of a spring plant that flowers in Mulchgrove. Grants resistance to [Poison damage](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#DamageTypes) when chewed for 1 hour.

18. **Wayfarrow:** Desiccated red berries of a thorny bush. Removes one level of [Exhaustion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#ExhaustionCondition) when chewed. *Psychedelic.*

19. **Witch's Oyster:** Miniature, bright violet oyster mushrooms that grow in high tree branches. Casts [Augury](http://dnd2024.wikidot.com/spell:augury) when boiled and drunk. *Psychedelic.*

20. **Wolfsbane:** Long sprigs of purple flowers. Shapeshifters have Disadvantage on attacks against wearer when applied to skin or clothes for 1 hour.
